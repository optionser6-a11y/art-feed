One-time setup (about 1 minute)
  1. Open Netlify, then your Nahim site, then Project configuration, then Environment variables.
  2. Add a variable named DASH_KEY. For the value, type a long password of your own (8+ characters).
  3. Go to Deploys, then Trigger deploy, then Deploy site. Wait until it says Published.
  4. Come back here and enter that password.
